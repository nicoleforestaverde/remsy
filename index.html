Parts of the day

Most apps make you
invent a minute.

Every other reminder app turns "this evening" into 19:00 before it will take it. Remsy keeps the answer you gave, then picks the minute for you inside that part of the day and rounds it to something tidy.

Every window is yours to move in Preferences, and a preferred time pins the part to one minute.

Or name the minute

The precision
is a range.

Pick a day and nothing else, set a preferred hour for each part of the day, hold weekends to a narrower window, or name the exact minute. A reminder set to 7:15 arrives at 7:15, and nothing rounds it.

On repeat

Every thirty minutes.
Every four hours.
Every year.

A number and a unit: minutes, hours, weeks, months or years. Minutes and hours run round the clock, which is what a course of medication needs. Any reminder can be set to come through a Focus mode, and none does unless you ask. Pause any repeat until a date you pick, and it starts again on its own with nothing to repair.

Roll it forward

Later. And later.
And later again.

One tap moves a reminder to the next part of your day, and it tells you where that lands before you take it. Swipe a card left to complete it. Miss one entirely and nothing happens: it stays in the list, still counted at the top of the screen, until you deal with it.

It arrives once

At its time, and then it stops. There is no second ping, no escalation and no red badge growing in the corner.

Someday

No date, but you do not want to lose it. Remsy draws a day between 15 and 90 out, spread apart so a handful shelved together do not all come back at once.

Share anything in

A recipe, an article, a link. Send it to Remsy from any app and it lands on the Someday shelf without asking you anything.

Make it yours

Nineteen palettes,
light and dark.

Same app, your choice of colour. Every one is measured rather than picked by eye, so the text holds its contrast on all of them. Tap one to try it.

Keep it private

No account.
No server of ours.

Your reminders live on your phone and are never transmitted. There is no sign-up, no analytics and no tracking. Each launch asks RevenueCat whether Remsy Plus is active on your store account, carrying an anonymous ID and your store receipt and nothing else.

Back up

To a plain file you keep, wherever you want it.

Restore

From that file on a new phone, after a confirmation.

Seven languages

English, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Korean and Japanese.

Local notifications

Scheduled on the device. No push service, no device token, nothing sent anywhere.
